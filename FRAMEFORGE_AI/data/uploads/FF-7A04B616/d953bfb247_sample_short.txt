THE LAST REEL
Written by Test Author

FADE IN:

INT. COFFEE SHOP - DAY

A small corner cafe. Rain taps the window. MAYA (30s, tired eyes, red scarf) sits alone, a film canister on the table.

BARISTA
(calling out)
Large black, for Maya!

MAYA
Thanks.

She slides the canister into her bag. Her PHONE buzzes.

EXT. CITY STREET - DAY

Maya hurries along a crowded pavement. A yellow TAXI splashes past.

MAYA
(into phone)
I'm ten minutes away. Tell Dev to wait.

INT. OLD CINEMA - LOBBY - DAY

Dust and faded posters. DEV (40s, projectionist, cardigan) unlocks a heavy door.

DEV
You brought it?

MAYA
Every frame.

DEV
Mr. Rao said it was lost forever.

INT. OLD CINEMA - PROJECTION ROOM - NIGHT

A vintage projector hums. Dev threads the film. Maya watches, hands trembling.

MAYA (V.O.)
My father shot this the summer before he disappeared.

The reel flickers to life: a smiling man on a beach.

EXT. OLD CINEMA - ALLEY - NIGHT

Maya steps outside for air. A black SEDAN idles at the end of the alley, headlights off.

MAN IN COAT (O.S.)
Miss Maya. We should talk.

She backs toward the door.

INT. OLD CINEMA - PROJECTION ROOM - LATER

Dev blocks the doorway with a fire axe prop from the lobby.

DEV
Go. Take the film. I will hold them.

MAYA
Dev, no.

EXT. BEACH - DAWN

Waves. Maya, in the same red scarf, unwraps the last frames and holds them to the rising sun. Silence.

FADE OUT.
